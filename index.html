What's in the app

Built around two people, not one to-do list.

Smart partner matching

We pair you by goal type, time zone, and how often you want to check in, so your buddy actually gets it.

Monthly check-ins

A guided video or chat check-in each month to review progress, reset goals, and plan the next stretch.

Progress tracking

Log daily wins, watch your streak grow, and see your buddy's progress right next to yours.

Goal-setting resources

Templates for SMART goals, habit stacking, and weekly reviews, written by coaches and real users.

Gentle nudges

Miss a day? Your buddy gets a prompt to send encouragement, not guilt.

Web dashboard

See your goals on a bigger screen. Everything syncs with the app, so you can check in from your laptop too.

How it works

From download to your first win in a week.

Set a goal

Pick a category and describe what "done" looks like. We help you make it specific.

Get matched

Within 48 hours you're paired with a buddy who has a similar goal and schedule.

Check in

Log progress daily or weekly. Your buddy sees it and can react or reply.

Review monthly

Look back together, celebrate what worked, and set the next target.
